ASHEN WILDS

Hunt or starve

Your hunger never stops. Bring down beasts with blade and spell, eat what you kill, and survive as many days as you can. You start at your cabin, where you are safe. Glowing waygates lead to the Verdant Vale, the Frostfang Tundra and the Cinder Wastes — each with its own beasts. In battle stance a target node marks the creature you are facing, and the camera locks onto whatever you attack; in normal stance your gear is put away and you travel lighter.
